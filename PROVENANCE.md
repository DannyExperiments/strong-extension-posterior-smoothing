# Provenance

The original Q432 proof packet contains a three-time 3-SAT reduction for an input rational threshold. The positive-row supplement already gives a smoothed construction with an input-dependent cutoff. The revised manuscript derives a fixed cutoff of one half, keeps it under strict positivity, and verifies the lower comparison independently after mixing creates extra initial states. The exact replay covers both endpoint choices and the transformed full rows.
