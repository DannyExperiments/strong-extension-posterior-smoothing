# Claim scope and limitations

The theorem applies to separately specified rational, time-indexed finite HMMs with local credal polytopes given by constraints or vertex lists. It asks for the posterior of one hidden-state value conditional on a partial assignment of manifest values with positive lower probability. The existential upper and lower comparisons are NP-complete for strict and non-strict cuts, and their complements are coNP-complete. Computing either exact tight bound is NP-hard.

Hardness persists at a fixed cutoff of one half using three hidden times, one final binary observation, strictly positive local entries and evidence probability uniformly at least one quarter. The hidden alphabet grows with the formula. No constant-size bound on the hidden alphabet above the fully binary case is proved. The smallest positive entries may depend on the input size. Stationary parameter tying, epistemic irrelevance and more general observation events are outside the claim.
